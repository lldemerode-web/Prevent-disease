How to play

Two fictional diseases spread between connected cities. Time runs automatically. Pause to plan, select cities, and invest in testing, hospital capacity, prevention, and travel screening. Research each disease to slow its spread globally. Win when both outbreaks are eliminated; lose if public trust reaches zero. This is a strategy game, not a medical model.
