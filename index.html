How to play

You lead a fictional international health response. Two diseases spread between cities and within populations. Confirmed cases undercount true infections. Testing improves detection, hospitals reduce deaths, prevention reduces transmission, and research develops countermeasures. Every action has a cost and policies affect public trust and the economy. The game starts paused; select a city, then press Start.

Goal: Contain both outbreaks for 14 consecutive days with fewer than 1,000 active infections globally. Lose: Public trust reaches zero, deaths exceed 2% of the world population represented, or the economy reaches zero. This is a game simulation, not an epidemiological forecast.
